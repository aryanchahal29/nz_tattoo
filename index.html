Our methodology

HOW WE WORK WITH YOU

1. Consult

We sit down in a private room to review placement, scale, reference artwork, and anatomy. Transparent quote provided upfront.

2. Design

Your artist draws your piece specifically for your body lines. We review and adjust until the stencil is exact.

3. The Session

Private, hygienic suites. Fresh single-use needles and hospital-grade aseptic setup. Work at your pace with regular breaks.

4. Settled Healed

Hospital-grade second-skin dressing, comprehensive print aftercare guide, and one complimentary touch-up within six months.
